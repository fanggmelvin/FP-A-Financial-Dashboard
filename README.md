
# FP&A Financial Modeling & Power BI Dashboard

### Excel Financial Modeling | Power BI | DAX | Financial Analysis

## Project Overview

Developed an end-to-end Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) project using Microsoft Excel and Power BI to analyze historical financial performance, evaluate budget variances, forecast revenue, and model financial scenarios.

This project demonstrates financial modeling, data transformation, financial reporting, and executive-level business analysis using historical sample financial data and illustrative planning assumptions.

### Project Objectives

- Analyze historical revenue, costs, and profitability.
- Compare actual performance against an illustrative budget.
- Identify monthly revenue trends and financial variances.
- Develop a forward-looking revenue forecast.
- Evaluate Bear, Base, and Bull financial scenarios.
- Communicate financial insights through interactive dashboards.

## 4. Financial Insights & Recommendations

### Revenue Performance

**Finding:** Historical revenue was 4.8% below the illustrative budget.

**Recommendation:** Investigate monthly revenue differences by product, business segment, and geography to understand the primary contributors to performance gaps.

### Revenue Seasonality

**Finding:** October and December recorded the highest monthly revenue.

**Recommendation:** Examine whether these peaks reflect recurring seasonal demand, promotional activity, or one-time transactions before incorporating similar patterns into future forecasts.

### Profitability Optimization

**Finding:** Historical gross margin was approximately 14.1%.

**Recommendation:** Analyze product-level margins, discounting practices, and cost drivers to identify potential opportunities for improving profitability.

These recommendations represent areas for further investigation rather than verified causes or realized financial improvements.

## 5. Financial Modeling Methodology

### Historical Financial Analysis

- Organized historical sample financial data in Microsoft Excel.
- Calculated revenue, COGS, gross profit, and gross margin.
- Aggregated financial performance by month.
- Analyzed monthly revenue and profitability trends.

### Budgeting & Variance Analysis

- Developed an illustrative financial budget.
- Compared actual revenue against budgeted revenue.
- Calculated absolute and percentage variances.
- Visualized monthly financial performance.

### Forecasting

- Developed a modeled 2015 revenue forecast using planning assumptions.
- Applied an illustrative gross margin assumption.
- Calculated projected gross profit.
- Compared forecasted monthly revenue against historical performance.

### Scenario Modeling

- Created Bear, Base, and Bull revenue scenarios.
- Applied scenario adjustments using DAX measures.
- Built interactive scenario selection in Power BI.
- Calculated scenario-adjusted revenue and gross profit.
